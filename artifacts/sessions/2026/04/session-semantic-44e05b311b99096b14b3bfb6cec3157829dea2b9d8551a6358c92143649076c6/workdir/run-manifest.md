## Decisions Made

- **Single lot, number "6".** The T&Cs body does not name a lot number (it is a framework-wide cleaning T&Cs template). `notice_lots` already carries a Lot 6 entry for Framework 6278, so keeping `lot_number = "6"` matches authority state and preserves resume keys. The long-form title is taken from the notice-level lot title; a dedicated warning records that the title is a notice-overlay fact, not a document fact.
- **Evaluation criteria and CPV codes emitted as empty arrays.** The draft T&Cs never state award criteria, weightings or CPV codes. Copying the framework-level CPVs (`50700000-2`, `50750000-7`, `71315000-9`) or inventing a cleaning-services CPV (e.g. `90910000-9` class) would violate the SKILL rule that every attribute must cite an evidence anchor in the document. Two dedicated warnings (`CPV_CODES_NOT_IN_DOCUMENT`, `EVALUATION_CRITERIA_NOT_IN_DOCUMENT`) point the downstream pipeline at the ITT/notice overlay to resolve them.
- **Four product-requirement seeds, categorical.** The T&Cs do not enumerate SKUs; they attach contractual obligations to categories of deliverable. Cleaning service, equipment, chemicals/materials, and KPI/reporting are the four distinct classes the clauses actually describe (B1, B2, D6.2, C3A + F5 + F7). Emitting one seed per class with stable `seed_key`s makes the seeds idempotent on retry and matches the Specification's likely carve-up.
- **Technical standards captured broadly.** BSI / ISO Quality Standards, Good Industry Practice, UK GDPR + DPA 2018, FOIA / EIR, PCR 2015, Public Procurement (Electronic Invoices etc.) Regulations 2019, Bribery Act 2010, Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, DBS regime (Protection of Freedoms Act 2012), H&S Law + Council policies, Equality Act 2010 (via D3), Local Government Act 1999 (Best Value), TUPE, Insurance minima (G1.7 / G2) and English Law jurisdiction (I1) are all emitted. This is consistent with the prior tender-specification session output for an analogous draft contract.
- **Schedule stubs flagged, not fabricated.** Page 28 contains heading-only blocks for Schedule 1 (Specification), Schedule 2 (Pricing Schedule), Schedule 3 (ITT) and Schedule 4 (Contractor's Tender). No bodies. No Docling tables. Fabricating service frequencies, Premises lists, Contract Price structures, or KPI tables would be ungrounded; instead the warnings `SCHEDULE_SPECIFICATION_STUB`, `PRICING_SCHEDULE_STUB`, `EVALUATION_CRITERIA_NOT_IN_DOCUMENT` and `KPI_AND_SERVICE_CREDITS_DEFERRED` point downstream at the separate specification pack.
