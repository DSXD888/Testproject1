## 4. Where I'd gently push back on your choices

### 4.1. Reconsider Disability Confident L1 + CRP baseline + no-Xinjiang

You only selected Living Wage from the social-value commitments round. Three reasons to revisit:

- **Disability Confident L1**: free, 30-min self-declaration. Adds scoring on some NHS + London-borough + central-gov bids. Zero downside.
- **CRP baseline**: only ~8 hours of work; CRP already exists in draft on Sorellon's website with TBC numbers, which is bid-defective. Completing it is a "finish what you started" exercise.
- **No-Xinjiang commitment**: I understand this might be a supply-chain flexibility decision. But NHS social-value scoring increasingly demands it. If you can't truthfully sign one, NHS PPE bids (your largest opportunity) become harder. Worth understanding why you said no — is it because your Chinese suppliers include Xinjiang sources you don't want to drop, or because you want optionality?

### 4.2. Strongly recommend a one-off paid BPR legal review

You chose in-house + AI for regulatory work. For most of it (MHRA UKRP verification, REACH importer setup, CLP labelling audit), in-house + AI is fine — these are procedural checklists.

**BPR is different.** Unauthorised biocidal product placement is a criminal offence with unlimited fines. HSE actively enforces post-COVID. AI cannot reliably interpret:
- Whether a specific active substance + product type combination has GB authorisation
- Whether transitional COPR cover applies to your supplier's product
- Whether marketing claims exceed authorisation scope

**Recommendation**: budget £500-£1,500 for a one-off legal/regulatory review of your AI-generated BPR audit before any biocidal SKU goes live. Specialist firms: RT Coopers, Compliance Gate, Casus Consulting.

### 4.3. Capital decision timing

You're deferring the capital decision until before bidding starts. Good in principle. **But**: by week 5 (insurance bind, first opportunity scouting), brokers and bid evaluators will ask about working capital. Don't let the decision drift past week 5 — make it part of the week 5 review cycle.

### 4.4. The "all 4 product categories simultaneously" stretch

You answered "all four" product categories for the first 90 days. Combined with China-direct import + biocidal claims, this means BPR audit for ~10-30 SKUs, MHRA UKRP verification for several distinct supplier categories, UKCA/CE verification per SKU, REACH importer setup, CLP labelling audit. **That's a lot for 60 days at 10-20 hours/week.**

Pragmatic alternative: SEQUENCE the categories. Weeks 1-6 focus on cleaning + janitorial (non-biocidal) + PPE (Cat I/II only). Healthcare consumables Class IIa and biocidal claims phase 2 — weeks 7-12. This still hits first-bid in 60 days but on lower-regulatory-risk SKUs.

Worth thinking about. Your call.
