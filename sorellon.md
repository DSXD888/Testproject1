## Company Overview

Sorellon Limited is a UK-based supplier of goods to the UK public sector and B2B buyers. The company sources and delivers high-volume essentials, including wipes, janitorial kits, healthcare consumables, cleaning and hygiene products, facilities supplies, painting and maintenance kits, and related operational consumables.

The company positions itself around straightforward, specification-led procurement where buyers need clear documentation, reliable delivery, responsive communication, and audit-ready evidence.

Core value proposition: **Fast. Compliant. No fuss.**

Public capability wording: Sorellon delivers reliable, value-for-money goods to government, healthcare, and education buyers, with compliant sourcing, transparent pricing, and fulfilment planned around buyer requirements and supplier confirmation.

Public note on client disclosure: Sorellon does not publicly disclose individual client names because of confidentiality agreements and public sector procurement protocols. The public capability statement says the team has experience fulfilling orders within NHS Trusts, local authorities, and education frameworks through direct supply and subcontracted partnerships. Do not name clients or present this as a formal case study without permission and evidence.

## RFQ And Fulfilment Process

1. **Send an RFQ** - buyer shares product, quantity, due date, delivery locations, and required standards/lab tests.
2. **Clarify specification** - Sorellon confirms product requirements, evidence needs, substitutions, delivery constraints, and evaluation criteria.
3. **Source and quote** - Sorellon returns price, estimated delivery lead time, technical notes, compliance pack scope, and samples if needed. Timing is case by case and depends on supplier/compliance confirmation.
4. **Approve and plan** - schedule is confirmed in writing, paperwork arranged, production or procurement booked, and key status updates agreed.
5. **Deliver and support** - UK delivery to one or many sites, lot codes recorded where relevant, documents stored for audit and recall support.
6. **Issue resolution** - single accountable contact handles replacements, clarifications, or delivery issues.

### Compliance Pack May Include

### VAT And ICO Notes Checked Online

VAT:

- HMRC says UK businesses must register for VAT if taxable turnover in the last 12 months is over GBP 90,000, or if taxable turnover is expected to go over GBP 90,000 in the next 30 days.
- HMRC also allows voluntary registration below the GBP 90,000 threshold.
- For a limited company VAT registration, HMRC says the company normally needs its company registration number, business bank account details, Unique Taxpayer Reference, annual turnover details, and estimated taxable turnover for the next 12 months.
- Practical tender position: do not claim VAT registration until a VAT number is issued. If a contract award would push taxable turnover over the threshold, VAT registration should be handled before invoicing and ideally with accountant support.

ICO/data protection fee:

- ICO says every organisation that processes personal information must pay a data protection fee unless exempt.
- ICO says companies that have not started trading do not need to pay a fee yet, but should retake the self-assessment once trading starts.
- Practical tender position: run the ICO fee self-assessment and search the ICO register before claiming a registration reference.
